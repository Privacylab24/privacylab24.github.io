What is Personal Data?

Personal data refers to any information that can identify an individual, either directly or indirectly. Examples include:

  • Name
  • Address
  • Date of Birth
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Purchase History
  • Sensitive Information (e.g., medical records, financial data)

Actionable Tip:

If in doubt, ask your privacy team before processing customer information. Protecting personal data is everyone’s responsibility!
